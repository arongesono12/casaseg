# Project Brief: Casaseg Mobile App

## 1. Overview
Casaseg is a real estate platform specialized in property rentals and sales in Equatorial Guinea. The mobile application aims to provide a seamless, trustworthy experience for tenants and owners to connect.

## 2. Target Audience
*   **Tenants:** Individuals looking for apartments, villas, or rooms in prime locations.
*   **Owners:** Property owners looking to publish and manage their listings efficiently.
*   **Communities:** Users seeking a reliable network within the local real estate market.

## 3. Brand Identity & Style
Based on the Casaseg web platform (casaseg.com), the mobile app follows these visual principles:
*   **Color Palette:** Signature deep blue (`#0056B3` approx.), vibrant orange/red acents for highlights, and soft pinkish-white gradients for backgrounds to create a modern, airy feel.
*   **Typography:** Bold, clean headings for clarity and high-quality sans-serif fonts for readability.
*   **Imagery:** High-resolution architectural photography of modern properties in Equatorial Guinea.
*   **Tone:** Professional, reliable, and welcoming.

## 4. Key Features & User Flows
### Phase 1: First Impressions (Completed)
*   **Splash Screen:** Branding and app initialization.
*   **Onboarding Flow:** 
    *   *Discover:* Introduction to the value proposition.
    *   *Trust:* Community statistics (Active properties, owners, plans) and social proof.
    *   *Action:* Clear path to "Get Started" or "Sign In".

### Phase 2: Core Functionality (Planned)
*   **Property Discovery:** Search with filters (location, price, type), map view, and featured listings.
*   **Property Details:** Image galleries, amenities, location maps, and direct contact options.
*   **User Profiles:** Management of favorites, saved searches, and account settings.
*   **Owner Dashboard:** Tools for listing creation and performance tracking.

## 5. Design Principles
*   **Clarity:** Use plenty of whitespace and clear hierarchy.
*   **Trust:** Surface data and verified community indicators.
*   **Simplicity:** Minimize friction in the search and contact process.
